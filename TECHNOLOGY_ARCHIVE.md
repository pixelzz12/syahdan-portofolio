## Editing and display

Edit `src/data/technologies.ts`: IDs, names, category, experience, logoPath, description, relatedProjectIds, visible and optional secondary categories. Entry order controls the archive order. `featuredTechnologyIds` selects six readable 3D cards per room. The full archive is inspectable from **Objek → Arsip teknologi**, and from each corresponding reading-mode section. Category filters permit browsing across rooms.

Development Bay covers web, programming, development tools and databases; Experimental Workshop covers electronics and IoT; Film Processing Lab covers design, photography and video/motion; R&D Chamber covers AI and homelab. Operator’s Quarters preserves the official UNAIR logo placeholder. Transmission Room preserves configured-contact validation.

MySQL has its recognizable licensed logo. SQL is a database language represented with readable text. Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva, Figma, Lightroom, Premiere Pro, After Effects and DaVinci Resolve have licensed SVG marks. CapCut awaits a verified licensed or official logo and displays initials. See `public/assets/logos/technologies/SOURCES.json` and `LICENSE.md`. No advanced professional expertise is implied.

All non-logo portfolio images are authentic placeholders until supplied. See `ASSET_CHECKLIST.md` and `ASSET_GUIDE.md`. EV is a visual robot; the local LLM assistant and homelab remain **Dalam Pengembangan**.
